AI Receptionist for Pakistani Businesses

Give callers a spoken way to reach the right team. An AI receptionist can be scoped around approved answers, opening hours and transfer rules.

Call workflow

  1. Answer and identify the enquiry
  2. Use the correct hours and routing rules
  3. Transfer or arrange a callback

Map the receptionist’s first questions

Define how the greeting identifies the business and how the agent separates appointment requests, billing questions and support calls. Keep instructions short enough to test with real caller phrasing.

Plan appointments and after-hours calls

Without an approved calendar connection, treat a booking as a request for staff to confirm. Keep separate rules for holidays, closed departments and callers who need a callback.

Build a human route into every workflow

Choose an extension, queue or callback route for exceptions. Test busy and unanswered transfers as well as the option to ask for a person early in the call.

Questions

AI Receptionist for Pakistani Businesses FAQs

Can callers speak instead of pressing a key?

Conversational AI accepts spoken requests. A keypad menu can still serve as a fallback or route callers to a fixed destination.

Can I update the receptionist’s instructions?

Define who owns opening hours, approved answers and routing rules. Agree the update process and rerun the affected test calls after a change.

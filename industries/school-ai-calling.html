AI Calling Agent for Schools and Colleges

Give families a clear telephone route to admissions information and staff, especially when application deadlines and campus visits generate extra calls.

Call workflow

  1. Ask about campus and grade
  2. Share published admissions information
  3. Connect the caller to the office

Give families campus-specific answers

Ask which campus, grade or programme the family is interested in. Use approved information about dates, fee schedules, documents and office hours. Send scholarship and exception enquiries to the admissions team.

Plan for peak admissions weeks

Route new applicants, existing parents and fee enquiries to different destinations. Agree callback ownership when lines are busy and publish separate rules for campus visits and school holidays.

Keep applicant follow-up scoped

Use approved contacts for admission follow-ups and collect only the information staff need to respond. Route student records, safeguarding concerns and individual decisions directly to authorized staff.

AI Calling Agent for Schools and Colleges FAQs

Can the agent confirm an admission decision?

Admissions decisions stay with authorized school staff. The calling agent can provide published information and route the enquiry.

Can parents ask about fees?

The workflow can use an approved, dated fee schedule for the relevant campus and programme. Staff handle account-specific balances and exceptions.

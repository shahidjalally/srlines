## Organize information by campus and intake

Callers may ask about several campuses, grades and programmes. Establish which intake and campus they mean before giving published dates, eligibility information or fee schedules. Assign a staff owner to maintain the answers each term.

## Separate general information from individual records

Public information can be shared over the phone. Personal records, individual admission decisions and payment issues need a staff process that checks the caller’s authority. Collect only the details needed for an authorized follow-up.

## Plan for busy weeks and exceptions

Test calls about deadline changes, scholarships and campus visits. Ask what happens if the admissions desk is busy. For follow-up calls, agree the purpose, contact permissions and how families can opt out.

## Keep the information administrative

Provide approved department hours, clinic addresses and appointment instructions. Use only a confirmed schedule to offer available times. When a connection to scheduling is absent, send a request to staff and explain that their confirmation is needed.

## Protect the privacy of callers

Use the minimum information staff need to arrange the appointment. Agree recording notices, staff access, retention and reminder preferences before going live. Review what an outbound reminder may say if another person answers.

## Design urgent-call handling separately

Use clear instructions to reach qualified human staff for clinical questions. The AI receptionist does not diagnose illness or prescribe medication. For a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

## Measure where calls go unanswered

Review abandoned and unanswered calls by hour, queue and reason. A useful baseline distinguishes calls outside office hours from calls that reached a busy team. Focus on the enquiries that otherwise leave the caller without a next step.

## Build overflow around ownership

An AI calling workflow can answer approved questions or capture a callback request, subject to your deployment. Define the number of rings before overflow, the team responsible for callbacks and the action when urgent or out-of-scope calls arrive.

## Measure the outcome after the pilot

Compare baseline and pilot periods with similar traffic. Track correct routing, successful callbacks and repeat calls as well as answer rates. Review samples with staff and adjust routing or information before rolling out to additional numbers.

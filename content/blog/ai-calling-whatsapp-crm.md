## Match a call to the right record

Start by defining how the workflow normalizes telephone numbers, handles shared numbers and deals with a caller who is not yet in the CRM. Where identity is ambiguous, route the record to human review before attaching call details.

## Choose the smallest useful handoff

Store a short call outcome and the next action where the business process requires them. A salesperson may need the product category and callback time. Access to recordings or transcripts can be scoped separately.

## Check WhatsApp permissions separately

Confirm that a WhatsApp follow-up is allowed and that the channel, templates and customer preferences support it. A phone enquiry should not be treated as unrestricted permission for messaging. Test duplicate prevention and what happens if a message fails.

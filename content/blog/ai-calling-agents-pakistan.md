## Start with a call your team already understands

An AI calling agent can respond to a spoken request using approved information and route calls that need staff. For a wholesaler, a good first flow is to ask which city and product category the caller needs, describe office hours and send a sales enquiry to the correct team. Capture the outcome for follow-up if your CRM workflow supports it.

## Test the telephone path before switching the number

Bring your current UAN or business number and carrier details to discovery. Ask how the number reaches the PBX, where a human transfer goes, and what happens if the agent or carrier is unavailable. For outbound calls, agree the contact purpose, permissions and opt-out handling before the pilot.

## Agree what a pass looks like

Use calls with Urdu and English phrasing, interruptions, bad audio, requests for a person and questions outside the approved knowledge. Track whether callers reached the correct destination and staff received usable context. Review mistakes before widening the topics or increasing traffic.

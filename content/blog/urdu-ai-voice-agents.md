## Test the way people actually speak

Ask for spoken test calls with common Urdu expressions, regional accents, English product names and mixed-language sentences. Include addresses, dates and prices. Written scripts alone miss background noise, interruptions and the sounds of place names.

## Confirm the details people rely on

If a caller gives a branch, appointment time or telephone number, have the workflow repeat the detail for confirmation. Where repeated attempts fail, route to a person or offer an agreed callback instead of guessing.

## Review performance by speech pattern

Record test outcomes by language, task and failure reason. A workflow that understands routine Urdu greetings may still need help with inventory codes or street names. Keep the wording and fallback criteria current as the business changes.

## Confirm service with the carrier

List your inbound number, potential outbound caller identity and intended peak concurrent calls. Ask the carrier about SIP delivery, codecs, service availability, permitted outbound use and pricing. Number portability and session limits depend on the service terms.

## Trace both directions

Walk an inbound call from carrier to PBX to AI or staff. Trace outbound calls back through the same plan, including caller ID and permission checks. Rehearse a failed AI session, a busy transfer and a congested trunk during a controlled pilot.

## Protect the migration

Test on a secondary number before moving the main number. Agree who can restore the previous call route, how quickly it can be restored and what signals trigger the change. Document support contacts and expected coverage hours.

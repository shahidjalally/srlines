## Build a monthly estimate

Start with the subscription and included usage. Add AI overage, carrier minutes and recurring charges for numbers, trunks, recording storage or support where applicable. Ask which taxes apply, what currency each line uses, and the basis and timing for currency conversions.

## Separate implementation and recurring charges

Implementation may include number provisioning, call-flow design, a CRM connection and acceptance testing. Ask which setup tasks are included, what a later workflow change costs and when subscription billing begins. A carrier contract may have its own commitments.

## Compare the same capacity and outcomes

If two providers quote different simultaneous-call limits, compare the same busy-hour demand, transfer behaviour and overflow. Ask whether queues or transfer legs consume sessions. Use a low and a busy usage scenario and request written assumptions for rates and allowances.

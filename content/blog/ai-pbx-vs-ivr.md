## Keep the routing distinction clear

A PBX connects business numbers, extensions and call routes. Traditional IVR can play a fixed menu and respond to keypad choices. A conversational voice application can use the caller’s own words to select a path or explain approved information.

## Use both tools where they fit

A fixed menu is useful for short, stable choices or a fallback during an outage. Spoken requests can be useful when callers describe a problem without knowing the department name. Define what should happen if both the first and second attempts fail.

## Test the common paths

Call during open and closed hours, ask for a person, enter an unexpected keypad digit and simulate a busy destination. Measure whether the correct team received the call and the caller knew what would happen next.

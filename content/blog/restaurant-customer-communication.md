## Answer branch-specific questions first

For a restaurant with several branches, confirm the location before giving menu, delivery or takeaway information. Use an approved menu and hours for each branch. Route allergy enquiries to staff whenever a reliable answer is unavailable.

## Give requests a clear status

Record a reservation request with branch, date, time and party size if the workflow supports it. The caller should know who confirms availability and when. Confirm an order or reservation only after staff or a verified booking or POS integration accepts it.

## Rehearse peak-hour exceptions

Test a branch that is busy, an unavailable menu item and a caller who asks for a manager. Give staff a way to update the approved information during service, and define who responds to callbacks after the rush.

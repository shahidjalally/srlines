Conversational AI and business telephony

SRLINES AI Calling Agent and AI-Enabled PBX is a wCRM-integrated business communication platform for Pakistani businesses. Conversational AI listens to a caller’s request and responds using business instructions. Traditional interactive voice response (IVR) plays prompts and routes by menu choice, including DTMF keypad input. A deployment can combine the two.

Inbound calls, outbound workflows and human handover

The agent handles inbound and outbound calls in Urdu and English, transfers callers to staff and integrates with wCRM. Scope individual workflows for receptionist enquiries, lead qualification, order questions, appointment requests and follow-ups. Agree approved answers and escalation rules, then test conversational context and any booking or CRM actions.

PBX routing, recording and reporting

SRLINES PBX supports layered IVR, team extensions, routing, managed SIP connectivity, call recording and reporting. Review queue behaviour, call history visibility, softphones, agent access and peak simultaneous calls during discovery. Configure the route when a destination is busy or unavailable.

How the pieces connect

In a common deployment pattern, a local business number reaches an Asterisk-based PBX through a supported SIP trunk. A real-time speech service, such as the OpenAI Realtime API, can provide the conversational layer where configured. The PBX routes calls to an IVR, an AI voice workflow or a human extension. A conversational voice service handles speech in real time. Work with SRLINES to confirm the deployment architecture, tenant boundaries, access and what call activity is passed to wCRM.

Pakistani UAN, SIP and carrier terms

Discuss whether your existing business UAN or a new number can be connected. Number availability, inbound and outbound routes, concurrent sessions and charges depend on carrier terms and the selected package. Confirm compatibility before porting or publishing a number.

Deployment and onboarding

Map call intent, hours, escalation, consent and staff ownership first. Then test SIP connectivity, a small set of AI scenarios, fallbacks, peak load and the wCRM handoff before routing live traffic. Agree a rollback route for launch.

Recording, consent and data handling

Agree which calls are recorded, how callers are informed, who can access call data, how long it is kept and when staff should take over. Use authorized outbound lists and a documented opt-out process. Review privacy and applicable requirements before going live.

Quotation and usage

Request a scope covering one-time implementation, monthly subscription, included usage, additional AI usage, carrier charges, concurrency, taxes and any exchange-rate-dependent rates. The signed quote defines the commercial terms.

AI Calling Agent and PBX FAQs

What is an AI Calling Agent?

An AI calling agent is a voice assistant that responds to spoken requests using business instructions and can route an enquiry to staff under agreed rules.

What is an AI-enabled PBX?

It is a business phone system that connects extensions, routing and other PBX functions with conversational AI voice workflows.

How does conversational AI differ from IVR?

Conversational AI uses spoken requests. Traditional IVR routes callers using prerecorded prompts and a fixed menu, often through keypad input.

Can the AI answer Pakistani telephone calls?

Pakistani inbound calls can be considered where the chosen carrier and number provide a supported SIP route to the PBX.

Can it make outbound calls?

Yes. SRLINES supports outbound AI calls. Availability for your number depends on the carrier, caller ID setup, applicable permissions and calling purpose.

Does it support Urdu?

Yes. SRLINES supports Urdu and English. Use your business phrases, accents and product names to test call quality before launch.

Can it transfer callers to human staff?

Yes. SRLINES supports transfer to human staff. Choose the destination and test overflow and callbacks before launch.

Can it connect with a business UAN?

SRLINES reviews UAN connectivity with your carrier. Feasibility and migration depend on number and package terms.

Can it work with WhatsApp CRM?

Yes. SRLINES AI Calling Agent and PBX integrates with wCRM. Confirm which call records, contact matches and follow-up steps are part of your deployment.

Can it record calls?

SRLINES PBX supports call recording. Agree when recordings are enabled, how callers are informed and who can access them.

How are concurrent calls managed?

The carrier and platform capacity determine how many calls can run at once. Test overflow and how transfers count before going live.

How is AI calling usage charged?

Ask for a quote separating AI billing units and overage from SIP or outbound rates, subscriptions, setup and taxes.

Can businesses customize the AI instructions?

Define approved business information, destinations and escalation rules during discovery and test them with realistic calls.

Can restaurants, hospitals and schools use the platform?

These organizations can scope administrative call workflows such as enquiries and routing, with staff handling exceptions and sensitive decisions.

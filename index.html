Questions from international buyers

Before we start.

Where is SRLINES based?

SRLINES is based in Sadiqabad, Punjab, Pakistan and works with organisations in Pakistan and internationally.

Who owns platform accounts and project deliverables?

Account ownership, access, source code, intellectual property and licences are recorded in the proposal or contract. For WhatsApp projects, we plan around customer-controlled Meta assets where the platform permits.

Can you guarantee Meta verification or an AI outcome?

No. We can assist with setup, integration and submission preparation, but Meta controls its decisions and automation outcomes depend on data, systems and agreed review controls.

How do remote projects stay visible?

The agreed proposal identifies milestones, review points, communication channels, responsibilities and acceptance criteria.

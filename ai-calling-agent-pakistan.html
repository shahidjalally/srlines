AI Calling Agents for Business in Pakistan

An AI calling agent listens to spoken requests and responds using business instructions. SRLINES supports inbound and outbound calls in Urdu and English, transfers to human staff, and integrates with wCRM through its AI-enabled PBX.

Call workflow

  1. Caller explains the request
  2. AI uses approved business instructions
  3. Staff handle exceptions

Inbound enquiries and lead qualification

Start with enquiries that have reliable answers. For a distributor, the agent could ask about the caller’s city and product category, give approved information and route a wholesale enquiry to sales.

Outbound calls with a defined purpose

SRLINES supports outbound calling for flows such as requested callbacks, confirmations or follow-ups. Agree who may be contacted, caller identification, opt-out handling, retry limits and transfer behaviour before enabling the workflow.

Urdu, English and mixed-language trials

Bring examples of real phrasing, product names, addresses and numbers to the demo. Test Urdu, English and code-switching with your callers’ accents and agree when a call should go directly to staff.

Questions

AI Calling Agents for Business in Pakistan FAQs

How does the agent handle an unfamiliar request?

Agree a fallback such as a human transfer, a callback request or a message for staff when the answer falls outside its instructions.

Can I trial it with a Pakistani business number?

SRLINES assesses the number, SIP handoff and carrier terms during discovery. Test calls and routing before moving your live number.

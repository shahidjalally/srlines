What each plan includes

AI telephone receptionist

Answers routine questions, captures enquiries and takes booking, appointment or order requests. Configured rules can route exceptions to your team.

Business calling identity

A DID is the number customers dial. A CLI is the authorised caller ID shown on outbound calls. Licensed telecom operators control allocation and use, subject to verification, approval and availability. A specific number or permanent ownership is not guaranteed.

Self-service web portal

Manage AI prompts, business knowledge and automation workflows. See allocated and remaining minutes, plan expiry and available activity reports.

Shared call capacity

Plans use a shared pool of simultaneous SIP call sessions. Fair use, network availability and shared capacity apply. Dedicated simultaneous lines are outside the package.

Questions

AI PBX pricing FAQs

How are inbound AI minutes counted?

Each connected AI session uses the plan’s inbound AI allowance and is rounded up to the next full minute per call.

Do unused minutes roll over?

Unused minutes expire at the end of each prepaid 30-day cycle without rollover or refund. Service may pause when the allowance or prepaid credit runs out.

Are outbound calls included?

Outbound calls are billed separately unless included by written agreement. Charges cover AI processing, applicable operator airtime, taxes and destination-based charges. SRLINES confirms the rates before activation.

Can I activate any number or outbound caller ID?

Activation requires operator approval, customer verification (KYC), a lawful business purpose and availability. Confirm number allocation and outbound caller-ID use before publishing a number.

Service terms and safeguards

Billing and renewals

Pay for each 30-day cycle in advance. Package totals are shown in PKR. Applicable taxes and any additional third-party charges are extra. The setup fee is non-refundable once implementation starts. Material changes and custom work require separate written approval.

Pricing changes

Prices, included minutes, extra-minute charges and service conditions may change because of exchange rates, inflation, taxes, operator tariffs or AI-provider pricing. SRLINES communicates changes before the next renewal. Existing prepaid periods remain subject to applicable law and agreed terms.

Consent and acceptable use

Customers are responsible for lawful calling lists, consent, marketing restrictions, notices and recording permissions. Spoofing, spam, fraud, harassment and unlawful automated dialling are prohibited. SRLINES may suspend misuse or act on regulator or operator instructions.

AI and data handling

AI can give inaccurate answers. Review prompts, business information and high-impact workflows. Minimise sensitive personal or medical information and manage authorised portal users. The infrastructure and AI providers needed to deliver the service process data under applicable policies.

Availability and liability

Shared telecom capacity, networks, operators and AI services can experience interruptions. Call-answer rates, uptime, sales outcomes and suitability for emergency services are not guaranteed. As permitted by law, liability is limited to fees paid for the affected service in the preceding 30 days, and indirect losses are excluded.

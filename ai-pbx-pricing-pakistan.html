Call workflow

  1. Estimate simultaneous calls
  2. Separate setup from recurring charges
  3. Confirm carrier and AI billing terms

One-time implementation

Ask for separate line items for discovery, number and SIP setup, call-flow configuration, business instructions, integrations, testing, staff onboarding and migration.

Subscriptions, AI usage and telephony

A quote should specify monthly charges, included minutes or resources, AI billing units, overage, outbound carrier rates and any number or trunk charges. Confirm how taxes and exchange-rate-dependent charges are handled.

Compare like-for-like capacity

For a monthly comparison, add subscription, AI usage beyond allowances, carrier charges and taxes. Compare both systems at the same busy-hour concurrency, support coverage and recording or storage requirements. Implementation remains a separate one-time cost.

AI Calling Agent and PBX Pricing in Pakistan FAQs

What is a concurrent call session?

It is a call active at the same time as another call. Ask how AI calls, queued calls and transfers count against carrier and platform capacity.

Can I get a price before choosing a carrier?

Request an indicative scope first. Confirm rates, available numbers and outbound terms once the carrier and package are established.
